# Free email templates to try

A collection of free HTML email templates, grouped by category in the [catalog](./catalog/food-and-recipes.md). Open any HTML template right in the browser, download the zip archives, or jump to the source file on GitHub.

:::note

We didn't create or own these templates. All credit goes to the developers and companies who made them and shared them for free. Some may be outdated or not render perfectly in every email client.

:::
